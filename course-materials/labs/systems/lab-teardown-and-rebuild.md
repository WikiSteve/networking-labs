## Summary

This reusable in-class practical checkoff sheet supports a group hardware teardown and rebuild activity. It functions more like a rubric or station worksheet than a full lab guide. The document lists the core tasks learners must complete and be marked on: tearing down a machine, rebuilding it, entering BIOS, locating the boot order, and answering one homework question. It also includes a strong warning not to remove the heatsink from the CPU, indicating that the activity is meant to build hands-on component familiarity while minimizing hardware risk.
