## Summary

This reusable submission template defines the five required screenshots for the Storage and IPv6 challenge. It does not teach the procedures directly. Instead, it standardizes the evidence package: RAID design proof, TrueNAS rebuild proof, Windows iSCSI proof, Cloudflare WARP DoH traffic proof, and IPv6 plus whois proof.

## Key Details

- It requires five screenshots in total.
- Screenshot 1 captures the RAID volume naming and workload-fit justification.
- Screenshot 2 captures the TrueNAS hostname, replacement action, and rebuild state.
- Screenshot 3 captures the 42 GB iSCSI-backed Windows mount, label, and IQN naming.
- Screenshot 4 captures Wireshark proof of Cloudflare WARP DoH destination, IP range, ownership, and computer name.
- Screenshot 5 captures the IPv6 source and destination plus whois confirmation of Cloudflare ownership.
- The deck functions as a grading-oriented evidence checklist rather than an instructional walkthrough.
- It pairs directly with the challenge note and keeps the submission structure consistent.
