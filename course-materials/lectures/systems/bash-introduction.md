This lecture deck introduces basic Bash usage. It covers `stdin`, `stdout`, `stderr`, redirection, `tee`, variables, `read`, exit codes, and a simple `if` statement, then finishes with a small `hostcheck.sh` example.

- Covers Bash input and output basics.
- Covers redirection and `tee`.
- Covers variables and `read`.
- Covers exit codes and simple `if` logic.
- Ends with a small `hostcheck.sh` example.
