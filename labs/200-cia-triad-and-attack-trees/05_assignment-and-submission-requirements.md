# Assignment and Submission Requirements

Keep the submission simple:

1. download the PowerPoint template from the README
2. complete the required slides
3. submit one finished `.pptx` file

## What goes on each slide

### Cover sheet

- put your name somewhere on the title slide

### Slide 1

- complete the first four Part A scenarios

### Slide 2

- complete the remaining Part A scenarios

### Slide 3

- paste your exported ADTool PNG

## Part A: CIA Triad Classification

For each scenario:

1. choose one classification:
   - confidentiality
   - integrity
   - availability
   - business process
2. choose an impact rating:
   - low
   - moderate
   - high
3. write a one-sentence justification

The template already includes the scenarios for you.

## Part B: Attack Tree in ADTool

Build an attack tree for:

- **gaining access to a house**

Requirements:

1. create the tree in ADTool
2. use a clear root objective
3. include multiple branches
4. show at least one dependent path
5. include at least one countermeasure
6. include at least one attacker response to a countermeasure
7. export the finished tree as a PNG image
8. paste that PNG onto Slide 3 of the template

## What will be graded

### Part A worth 5 marks

You will be graded on:

- correct classification
- reasonable impact rating
- a justification that matches the scenario

### Part B worth 5 marks

You will be graded on:

- a sensible attack-tree structure
- correct use of alternative versus dependent branches
- inclusion of countermeasures or attacker responses
- a clear exported PNG included in the document
